> Generated by `analysis/m8b/summarize_m8b.py`; the data for the new arms is `per_event.csv`,
> the STOCK / GATED / MAHAL arms are **quoted directly from the M8 `data/m8_replay/per_event.csv`, not re-run**.

> reference `/rtk_odom`. Peak = largest deviation within 2 s after the event (m).

> **MEAS+NODE** = `ws_gate` node-side gate + measurement-side gate. The gate rule and parameters of both are identical word for word to GATED.
