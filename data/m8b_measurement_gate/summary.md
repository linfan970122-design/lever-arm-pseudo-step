# M8b — replay results with the gate moved to the **measurement side** (EKF input)

> Data sources: `per_event.csv` in this directory (368 rows for the new arms), `gate_equivalence.csv`, `natural_events.csv`,
> `runs/` (24 replays); Tables 1-4 are generated by `analysis/m8b/summarize_m8b.py` (verbatim in `summary_tables.md`),
> Tables 5-8 by `natural_events_m8b.py` / `equiv_m8b.py` / `baseline_cost_m8b.py` / `verify_rate_m8b.py`.
> **The STOCK / GATED / MAHAL arms were not re-run**; they are quoted directly from the M8 `data/m8_replay/per_event.csv`.
> The design and the acceptance criteria were written before the runs: `design.md`.

## Conclusion in one sentence

**With the gate on the measurement side all four gain settings are stopped, and the decision is completely independent of the gain.**
For 90° injections the consumer peak deviation of MEAS-GATE at q=0.06 / 1e-3 / 1e-4 / 1e-5 is
0.002–0.007 / 0.002–0.007 / 0.006–0.007 / 0.005–0.006 m respectively (median, for 1/2/5 frames),
whereas in M8 the node-side gate fails completely at q=1e-5 (0.088 / 0.166 / 0.295 m, the same level as the STOCK 0.085 / 0.167 / 0.282 m).
The gate decision counts are **identical frame by frame** across the four q settings (accept 9657±, HOLD 56, withheld 144, see Table 6),
which is exactly what the hypothesis asked for: **the measurement-side criterion does not pass through the filter gain, so no gain setting can break it**.

Cost: (1) outside the 45 injections the measurement-side gate keeps 92 frames from the EKF (**0.93 %** of 9860 frames) and hands over a stale heading on 9 frames;
(2) but the consumer `/odometry/gps` **loses no frame at all** (position withheld 0), while the node-side gate drops 5–60 frames;
(3) the apparent baseline cost (p99 0.114–0.122 m against the STOCK 0.017 m) is **almost entirely the reference going bad itself** —
see Table 7: after removing the 10 segments where the recorded heading is frozen or 0, the MEAS p99 = 0.0168 m against STOCK 0.0162 m.

🔴 **Do not stack the two gates**: MEAS+NODE is no better on the same injections (Tables 1/2 put it at the same level as MEAS),
and the baseline is worse (p99 0.284–0.335 m; a single 0.73 m point remains even after the bad segments are removed, Table 7).
The cause can be followed frame by frame at 1545–1553 s: the measurement-side gate has already corrected the filter heading back,
the node-side gate treats that **correction** as a fault and latches `hold → stale` for 10 s before `reanchor_two`
(same window: STOCK 0.028 m, MEAS alone 0.369 m, GATED alone 0.478 m, the two stacked 0.731 m).

## Table 6 🔴 Equivalence: online gate vs `fig/gate.py`, **identical frame by frame**

## Table 8 Rate verification (a new Python node is in the loop, so verify once more)

The criterion follows §7 of the M8 `design.md`: **the difference introduced by fast replay must not exceed the difference between repeats at the same rate** (a 1 mm criterion is unreachable on this chain at any rate).
Same arm (MEAS, q=0.06, no injection), same window (bag 1100–1220 s), only `-r` changed:

| Comparison | Median (m) | p99 (m) | Max (m) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6× vs 6× (baseline) | 0.000002 | 0.0144 | 0.0258 |
| 1× vs 6× (a) | 0.000001 | 0.0107 | 0.0259 |
| 1× vs 6× (b) | 0.000000 | 0.0109 | 0.2095 |

The 0.2095 m in (b) sits in the **first 3 frames** of the comparison window (the two replays started 0.4 s apart, so the filter had converged to a different degree);
after dropping the first 2 s of the common window: 6× vs 6× p99 0.0144 / max 0.0258; 1× vs 6× p99 0.0091–0.0108 / max 0.0200–0.0259.
**The criterion is met**, 6× stays in use.

## Not yet tested

- Vehicle in the loop: everything here is replay (same as M8).
- The measurement-side gate in a **multi-sensor** setting (here the EKF has only one absolute heading source; with a second absolute heading source, withholding a frame has different consequences).
- HOLD frames hand a "stale but steady" heading to the EKF as a measurement: HOLD frames are rare in this recording (9 outside injections), so the consequence of a long HOLD during a turn was not exercised.
- The ROS 2 version of `robot_localization`; the heading entry point behind `use_odometry_yaw:=true`.
- The gate parameters were not re-swept (the GATED 17.0°/frame is reused); the optimal budget on the measurement side may differ from the node side.
