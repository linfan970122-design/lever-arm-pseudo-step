## The "+100 deg is purely cross-track" sentence

delta = -2*phi = +100.0 deg  ->  a = +0.00e+00 m, c = -0.5600 m  (along-track vanishes).
delta = +2*phi = -100.0 deg  ->  a = +0.5515 m, c = -0.0971 m  (NOT purely cross-track).

Under the convention the data supports (delta = -Delta_h), a purely cross-track step needs delta = +100 deg, i.e. a receiver heading jump of Delta_h = -100 deg. The manuscript sentence is therefore self-consistent with its own sign map.
